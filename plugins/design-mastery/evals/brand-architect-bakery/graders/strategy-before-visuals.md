The user asked for a logo and color palette but gave almost no information about the business.

PASS if, before committing to a final logo or palette, the response asks discovery or positioning questions about the bakery (for example: who the customers are, what makes it different from other bakeries nearby, its personality or values, its name, price point, or the feeling it should give) or clearly explains that these answers are needed first. Offering a few clearly provisional directions alongside the questions is acceptable.

FAIL if the response delivers a finished logo concept and a specific hex palette as the answer without asking any positioning or audience questions.
