Our landing page mockup feels "retro" and I can't say why. It has a black background, gold (#C9A227) accents, a sunburst of thin radiating lines behind the headline, stepped borders on the cards, chevron dividers, and a tall geometric sans-serif set in all caps. Where does this look come from, and how do we keep it from feeling like a costume?
