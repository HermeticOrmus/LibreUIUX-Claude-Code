Use Dieter Rams' design principles to simplify our app's settings screen. Right now it has: 42 toggles on one long page with no grouping, a decorative gradient header with an illustration, three different "Save" buttons in different places, tooltips on every label, and an "Advanced" section that most users never open but that sits at the top.
